Delete your DoorStride account

Last updated November 24, 2026

DoorStride is made by Doorstride Delivery Services. You can delete your DoorStride account, and the personal information that goes with it, at any time.

Delete it yourself (fastest)

  • Open the DoorStride app, or go to doorstride.expo.app in your phone’s browser, and sign in.
  • Go to Account, scroll down, and tap Delete my account.
  • Read what will be deleted, then tap Delete my account again. It happens right away and signs you out on every device.

If you have an order in progress, finish or cancel it first. Riders: remit any commission you still owe first.

Can’t sign in? Ask us

Email doorstride.official@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it’s done. You can also message us on Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/doorstride

What’s deleted

  • Your name, mobile number, email address and password
  • Your profile picture and saved places
  • Riders: GCash or Maya numbers, license, ID, motorcycle and OR/CR photos, and location
  • Chat messages you sent, and your notification settings

What’s kept, and for how long

  • Order and payment records (route, items, amounts, delivery photo and ratings) stay without your name, for as long as tax and accounting laws require, then they’re deleted.

You can sign up again later with the same email; it will be a new account.
