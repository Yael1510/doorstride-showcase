Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

This policy explains what personal information DoorStride collects, why, who can see it, and the choices and rights you have. We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and the rules of the National Privacy Commission. Doorstride Delivery Services is the personal information controller for the DoorStride app and website.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, mobile number, email address, password (stored encrypted; we can’t see it), and a profile picture if you add one. If you use Sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us (never your Google password).
  • Orders: pickup and drop-off addresses and map pins, landmarks, the recipient’s name and mobile number, notes, items to buy, fares, and payment method, promo codes used, and tips.
  • Saved addresses: places you save for quick booking, such as Home or Work.
  • Location: for riders, your phone’s location while you are online or on a delivery, so we can offer nearby orders and customers can follow their delivery. We keep only your latest position, and we check it for impossible jumps to keep order offers fair. For customers, your location only when you choose “My location” to set a pin, or tap “Share my current location” during an order so your rider can find you.
  • Proof of delivery: the photo of the delivered item, and the recipient’s signature if one was taken.
  • Problem reports: what you tell us when you report a problem with an order, and any photo you add.
  • Rider applications: driver’s license and license number, a valid government ID, motorcycle photos, the motorcycle’s registration (OR/CR), and plate number.
  • Rider payment details: the GCash or Maya number a rider chooses to add, so customers can pay them directly.
  • Store owner accounts: the store you manage, and the store details and products you update.
  • Chat messages and ratings between customers and riders.
  • Device information: a notification token for your phone or browser if you turn on alerts, and basic technical logs needed to keep the service secure.

2. Why we use it

  • To create your account and let you sign in.
  • To quote fares, match orders with riders, and complete deliveries (needed to fulfil our agreement with you).
  • To show live tracking, connect customers and riders by call or chat, and send order alerts.
  • To check that riders are qualified and safe before approving them, and to handle commissions and cash remittances.
  • To handle complaints, lost or damaged items, fraud, and safety issues (our legitimate interest in running a safe service).
  • To keep records required by tax and other laws.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t use it for advertising by other companies.

3. Who can see it

  • Riders see open orders without your name, phone numbers, or notes: only the pickup, drop-off, distance, fee, and items, so they can decide. Once a rider takes your order, they see the pickup and drop-off, notes, and the recipient’s name and number. Seven days after the order is delivered or cancelled, those names, numbers, notes, your shared location, and the chat are hidden from the rider again.
  • Customers see their rider’s name, plate number, and live location only while that rider is delivering their order, and the GCash or Maya number the rider added, so they can pay the rider directly.
  • A partner store you order from sees that order while it’s open and for 24 hours after it’s delivered or cancelled: the items, the delivery address, the recipient, and its status, so they can prepare it. It doesn’t see your past orders after that.
  • Our admin team sees orders, rider applications, and records to run the service and handle support.
  • Service providers we use to run DoorStride, only as needed: Supabase (database, sign-in, and file storage, hosted in Singapore); Expo (app hosting and notification delivery); your browser’s push service (for example Google, Apple or Mozilla) if you turn on browser alerts; Brevo (sends account emails, such as sign-up confirmation and password-reset codes; it receives your email address and the message); Google, only if you choose Sign in with Google; and Geoapify and OpenStreetMap (map images, address and landmark search, and route distances; they receive the map area or the text you search for, not your account details).
  • Government authorities, only when the law requires it or to protect someone’s safety.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account details: while your account is open. When you ask us to close it, we delete or anonymize your details within 30 days, except records we must keep.
  • Orders, payments, and proof of delivery: for as long as tax and accounting laws require, then deleted.
  • Rider documents: while you ride with us, and up to 90 days after you leave in case of open disputes.
  • Rider location: only the most recent position is stored; it is overwritten as you move.
  • What riders see: full order details while an order is open and for 7 days after it ends; after that, only the route, amounts, tip, rating, and delivery photo in their earnings history.
  • What partner stores see: an order only while it’s open and for 24 hours after it’s delivered or cancelled.

5. How we protect it

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access is limited by role: customers, riders, and admins can only read the records they need, enforced by the database itself. Proof photos and rider documents are kept in private storage and shown only to people allowed to see them. If a breach ever puts your information at risk, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used
  • access the personal information we hold about you
  • correct information that is wrong or out of date
  • object to processing, or ask us to delete or block your data when it is no longer needed
  • get a copy of your data in a common format (data portability)
  • be compensated for damages from unlawful use of your data
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph)

To use these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer below. We’ll reply within 15 working days.

7. Children

DoorStride accounts are for people 18 and older. We don’t knowingly collect information from children. A child may be named as a recipient by an adult customer.

8. Changes to this policy

We’ll post any changes here with a new date and tell you in the app about important ones.
