One app, four roles

Customers book. Riders deliver. You run the show.

Everyone uses the same app. What they see depends on who signs in.

For customers

Book in a minute, follow every step

  • Order from partner stores or drop pins anywhere on the map
  • Add a second or third stop to get things from several stores in one trip
  • See the fare before booking, add a promo code, pay cash on delivery
  • An alert at every step, live map and chat with the rider
  • Photo proof at drop-off, rate the rider, full order history
For riders

Go online, accept, deliver, earn

  • Apply with license, ID and motorcycle photos
  • A chime the moment a new order comes in; first to accept gets it
  • See what you'll earn before accepting, and exactly what cash to collect
  • Step-by-step status, call or message the customer
  • Earnings, delivery history, ratings and cash owed in one place
For the owner

See everything on one screen

  • Live map of riders and orders waiting for pickup
  • Approve riders after checking their documents
  • Search, edit or disable any account
  • Set fares and commission, draw your service area
  • List partner stores, give their owners a login, run promo codes
  • Daily reports and cash collected from riders
For partner stores

Orders in, menu up to date

  • A chime and the exact items to prepare for every order

Built to bring in orders, not just take them

The features that turn a delivery app into your town's go-to: local stores, launch promos, and nobody missing an order.

Partner stores

Restaurants, the market and pharmacies show on every customer's home screen. One tap to order, and the pickup is already set.

Several stores, one trip

Milk tea from one shop and lunch from another, in one booking. Each extra stop adds ₱15 plus the extra distance, and customers pick from each store's menu.

An app for every store

Store owners sign in to see their orders with a chime, switch sold-out items off, and close the store when they're busy.

Promo codes

Free delivery on a first order, pesos or percent off. You pay for the discount, so riders always earn their full share.

Instant alerts

Riders hear a chime the moment a new order comes in. Customers get a ping when the rider is found, picks up and arrives.

History for everyone

Customers see active and past orders. Riders see every delivery they made, what they earned, and how it was rated.

Menus by category

Each store's menu opens one category at a time, with a photo for every dish, so customers find what they want without endless scrolling.

Search as you type

Start typing a barangay, a store or a landmark, and places nearby pop up. Pick one, or drag the map to the exact spot.

Light or dark mode

A tap on the moon switches the whole app to dark mode, easy on the eyes for late night orders, and the app remembers the choice.

Made for Nueva Ecija

Set up for how deliveries work here

Simple, fair fares

₱60 covers the first 2 km, then ₱15 per km. Sample road trips from San Isidro:

ToDistanceFare
Around town2 km₱60
Jaen4 km₱90
Gapan6 km₱120

Cabiao10 km₱180

Ordering from more than one store? Each extra stop adds ₱15, plus the extra distance (up to 5 extra stops).

Your service area

Draw it on the map by tapping corners. Bookings outside it are blocked automatically.

Riders keep 75%

The commission is set by you. On cash orders the app tracks what each rider owes until they hand it over.

Landmarks, not just addresses

Every pin takes a landmark note like "beside the sari-sari store", because that's how people give directions.

"Pabili" built in

For food and item orders the rider buys for the customer, and the app adds the item cost to the cash collected at the door.

Private by design

Customers see only a rider's plate and location, and only during their own active delivery. Licenses and IDs stay between the rider and the owner. Chats close when the order ends.
