1. What DoorStride does

DoorStride is a platform that connects customers who need something picked up and delivered with independent local riders in our service area. We set the fares, match orders with riders, and provide tracking, chat, and support.

Riders are independent service providers, not our employees. They decide when to go online and which orders to accept.

  • One person, one account. Don’t share or sell accounts.
  • We may suspend or close accounts that break these terms, give false information, or put riders, customers, or partner stores at risk.

3. Booking, fares, and payment

  • The app shows the delivery fee before you book. It is based on the route distance: a base fare for the first kilometers plus a per-kilometer rate, as shown in the app. Each extra pickup stop you add (another store or place on the way) adds the extra-stop fee shown in the app, up to the number of stops the app allows.
  • DoorStride takes orders only during its service hours shown in the app. Pickup and drop-off must be inside our service area.
  • You can schedule an order for a later time. A scheduled order from a partner store must fall within that store’s opening hours and not on a day it is closed.
  • Give accurate pins, landmarks, and a recipient who can receive the order. If the rider can’t find the drop-off or reach the recipient, they will call or message you.
  • You pay the rider on delivery: the delivery fee plus the cost of any items in your order. You can pay in cash, or by GCash or Maya straight to the rider’s number when the app shows one.
  • Promo codes discount the delivery fee only. Each code has its own conditions shown in the app (for example first order only, a limit per customer, or an end date), can’t be exchanged for cash, and may be ended by us at any time. A promo never reduces what the rider earns.
  • Tips are optional and go 100% to your rider.

4. Store orders and pabili (buy-for-me)

  • Partner store orders: when you order from a partner store’s menu in the app, the store accepts your order and sets a ready time, or may reject it (for example if it is closed or out of stock). The rider pays the store for your items at the menu price, and you pay the rider back on delivery. The store is responsible for the quality, freshness, and safety of what it sells.
  • For pabili orders, the rider pays for your items at the store and you pay them back at the door. The item cost you enter is an estimate; you pay the actual amount on the store receipt.
  • If an item costs much more than your estimate, the rider will contact you before buying it.
  • Don’t ask riders to buy items they cannot legally buy for you (see section 5).
  • If an item is out of stock, the store or rider follows the choice you picked on the order: replace it, remove it, or call you first.

5. Items we don’t deliver

You may not book deliveries of:

  • illegal drugs, or prescription medicine without a valid prescription
  • firearms, ammunition, explosives, fireworks, or other weapons
  • flammable, toxic, or hazardous materials
  • stolen goods, counterfeit items, or anything illegal to own or transport
  • alcohol or tobacco for anyone under 18
  • live animals, human remains, or cash and valuables (jewelry, gadgets) packed to hide their value
  • anything too large, heavy, or unsafe to carry on a motorcycle

Riders may refuse or cancel an order that breaks this list. Orders involving illegal items may be reported to the authorities.

6. Cancellations and no-shows

  • You can cancel in the app only until your order is accepted, by the store or by a rider. Once accepted, the store starts preparing it, so it can’t be cancelled.
  • Two exceptions: you can still cancel if the store is more than 15 minutes past the ready time it gave and hasn’t marked your order ready, or if no rider has taken your order 15 minutes after it was opened to all riders.
  • If a store rejects your order, it is cancelled and you pay nothing. DoorStride may also cancel an order, for example if it breaks these terms or can’t be completed.
  • For pabili orders, you still owe the rider the item cost they paid.
  • If the recipient can’t be reached at the drop-off after the rider has waited and tried to call, the rider may end the delivery. Repeated no-shows may lead to account suspension.

7. Proof of delivery and ratings

Riders take a photo of the delivered item as proof of delivery, and may also ask for the recipient’s signature. After delivery, you can rate the rider. Ratings must be honest and respectful; we may remove abusive comments.

8. For riders

  • To ride with DoorStride you need a valid driver’s license for motorcycles (and its number), a valid government ID, a photo of your motorcycle, its registration (OR/CR), and its plate number, uploaded in the app. We review every application and may approve or decline it.
  • You earn the delivery fee minus DoorStride’s commission (currently 25%, rounded down to the whole peso, so you keep the centavos), shown in the app for every order. During the peak hours shown in the app you also get a peak-hour bonus, paid by DoorStride. Tips go 100% to you, and promo codes never reduce your earning.
  • Customers pay you directly, in cash or by e-wallet, so you hold DoorStride’s commission and must hand it to the admin every day at the end of the day, in person or by GCash. If what you owe reaches the cash limit shown in the app, you get no new orders until you remit.
  • When DoorStride closes for the day, riders still online are switched offline. A delivery already in progress isn’t affected.
  • Follow traffic laws, wear a helmet, and handle customers’ items with care. Keep customers’ phone numbers and addresses private and use them only for the delivery.
  • We may suspend riders for unsafe driving, unpaid commissions, fraud, poor conduct, or repeated cancellations.

9. For partner stores

  • A store owner account is linked to a store by DoorStride after the store signs our Partner Store Agreement. That signed agreement covers fees, orders, payments, and responsibilities; if it conflicts with these terms, the agreement wins.
  • Keep your menu, prices, item availability, opening hours, and closed days accurate in the app (Account → Store hours). Outside your hours, and on closed days, your store shows as Closed and takes no orders.
  • Accept or reject each new order within the time shown in the app, set a realistic ready time, and tap “Mark ready” when it is.
  • Use customers’ names, addresses, and numbers only to prepare and hand over their orders.

10. Respect and safety

Be respectful to riders, customers, partner stores, and our staff in person, on calls, and in chat. Harassment, threats, discrimination, or fraud will lead to suspension and, where needed, a report to the authorities.

Where the rider or DoorStride is at fault, we may refund the delivery fee or, for pabili and store orders, the item cost paid, or give you a credit. Where the store is at fault, the store replaces the item or refunds its price through us. Pack fragile items properly; we aren’t responsible for damage from poor packing.
