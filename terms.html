1. What DoorStride does

DoorStride is a platform that connects customers who need something picked up and delivered with independent local riders in our service area. We set the fares, match orders with riders, and provide tracking, chat, and support.

Riders are independent service providers, not our employees. They decide when to go online and which orders to accept.

2. Your account

  • You must be at least 18 years old to create an account.
  • Give your real name and a mobile number and email you can be reached on. Keep your password private; you are responsible for activity on your account.
  • One person, one account. Don’t share or sell accounts.
  • We may suspend or close accounts that break these terms, give false information, or put riders, customers, or partner stores at risk.

3. Booking and fares

  • The app shows the delivery fee before you book. It is based on the route distance: a base fare for the first kilometers plus a per-kilometer rate, as shown in the app.
  • Pickup and drop-off must be inside our service area.
  • Give accurate pins, landmarks, and a recipient who can receive the order. If the rider can’t find the drop-off or reach the recipient, they will call or message you.
  • Payment is cash on delivery unless another method is shown in the app. Pay the rider the delivery fee plus any item cost shown in your order.

4. Pabili (buy-for-me) orders

  • For pabili orders, the rider pays for your items at the store and you pay them back at the door. The item cost you enter is an estimate; you pay the actual amount on the store receipt.
  • If an item is unavailable or costs much more than your estimate, the rider will contact you before buying a replacement.
  • Don’t ask riders to buy items they cannot legally buy for you (see section 5).

5. Items we don’t deliver

You may not book deliveries of:

  • illegal drugs, or prescription medicine without a valid prescription
  • firearms, ammunition, explosives, fireworks, or other weapons
  • flammable, toxic, or hazardous materials
  • stolen goods, counterfeit items, or anything illegal to own or transport
  • alcohol or tobacco for anyone under 18
  • live animals, human remains, or cash and valuables (jewelry, gadgets) packed to hide their value
  • anything too large, heavy, or unsafe to carry on a motorcycle

Riders may refuse or cancel an order that breaks this list. Orders involving illegal items may be reported to the authorities.

6. Cancellations and no-shows

  • You can cancel in the app until the rider has picked up your order.
  • Once picked up, an order can’t be cancelled. For pabili orders, you still owe the rider the item cost they paid.
  • If the recipient can’t be reached at the drop-off after the rider has waited and tried to call, the rider may end the delivery. Repeated no-shows may lead to account suspension.

7. Proof of delivery and ratings

Riders take a photo of the delivered item as proof of delivery, and may also ask for the recipient’s signature. After delivery, you can rate the rider. Ratings must be honest and respectful; we may remove abusive comments.

8. For riders

  • To ride with DoorStride you need a valid driver’s license for motorcycles, the motorcycle’s registration (OR/CR), and the documents we ask for in the app. We review every application and may approve or decline it.
  • You earn the delivery fee minus DoorStride’s commission, shown in the app for every order.
  • For cash orders you collect the full amount, so you must hand DoorStride’s commission to the admin every day at the end of the day, in person or by GCash.
  • Follow traffic laws, wear a helmet, and handle customers’ items with care. Keep customers’ phone numbers and addresses private and use them only for the delivery.
  • We may suspend riders for unsafe driving, unpaid commissions, fraud, poor conduct, or repeated cancellations.

9. Respect and safety
